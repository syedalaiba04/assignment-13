## About this assignment

In this assignment, I worked with a teaching dataset containing prices for four items: Staple A, Oil B, Pulse C and Sweetener D.

I calculated the mean, median and percentage change for each item. I then compared the results to understand how the two averages can give us different information about prices. I also looked at an unusually high value and checked how it affected the mean and median.

The assignment includes my calculations, an AI interpretation of the results, my checking of that interpretation, and a short reflection on what I learned.

**Note:** The dataset is illustrative teaching data and does not contain actual Pakistan Bureau of Statistics observations.
